# General and Appearance

Personal settings for app behavior and look.

## General

- Open Settings, Personal, General.
- Review general app preferences for the desktop shell.
- Save changes so they persist in personal settings.

## Appearance

- Open Settings, Personal, Appearance.
- Adjust theme and visual preferences for the Agents UI.
- Confirm the chrome and workbench update to match your selection.
